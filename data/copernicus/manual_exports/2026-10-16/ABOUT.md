# Sentinel-2 browser views, Oct. 16, 2026 (the day before the fire)

Level-2A visualizations exported from the Copernicus Browser: individual bands B01–B09 and B8A, and standard composites (true colour, false colour, SWIR, NDVI, NDWI, NDSI, moisture index, wildfire). JPEG, 1250 × 858 px, same framing as the Oct. 4 exports (`../2026-10-04/`) at half the size; not georeferenced.

**What it shows.** Thin cloud and haze cover much of the scene, but the SWIR composite sees through it. The burn scars already present on Oct. 4 (south of Jamieson Lake and to the southwest) are visible, and **the ground north and east of Jamieson Lake, where the Oct. 17 burn was later measured, shows no burn on Oct. 16.** This is the last Sentinel-2 view before the fire and supports dating the main scar to Oct. 17. The small Oct. 14 burn on the lake's west shore cannot be made out reliably through the haze at this resolution.

**Probative value:** moderate (pre-fire state one day before; partly obscured).
