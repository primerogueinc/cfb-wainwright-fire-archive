# Sumerian Oil & Gas: ownership and the Cleo Energy receivership

Prime Rogue Inc., Nov. 3, 2026. A public data note built from court records, receiver filings, regulator orders and Alberta Gazette notices. Everything here is on the public record and is linked below. It makes no claim that any well or pipeline played a part in the Oct. 17 fire. Where something is our inference, we say so.

## Short version

- **Sumerian Oil & Gas Inc. is Cleo Energy Corp. under a new name.** Same Alberta corporation (corporate access number 2019731419, incorporated May 31, 2016). The name changed on Nov. 24, 2025.
- **No licence transfer took place.** A numbered company, **2698902 Alberta Corp.**, bought 100% of Cleo's shares out of receivership through a reverse vesting order (RVO). Cleo's old liabilities went to a new shell, **2755857 Alberta Ltd.** ("ResidualCo"), and the operating company kept its AER licences. The receiver said the structure let the buyer "avoid seeking AER approval for multiple licence transfers."
- **Who owns 2698902 Alberta Corp. is not public.** The share purchase agreement was signed for the buyer by an "Authorized Signatory." In the same agreement (s. 6.2(n)), the buyer represented that it "is a WTO investor within the meaning of the Investment Canada Act." Under that Act, a WTO investor is a non-Canadian (or an entity controlled by non-Canadians) from a WTO member country. We have not confirmed whether this is accurate or boilerplate.
- **The buyer did not pay a debt it took on to get the deal approved.** On the day of the first RVO hearing, the buyer agreed to pay Canadian Natural Resources Ltd. (CNRL) $88,233.15 in contract arrears within 30 days of closing. It did not pay. CNRL obtained a consent judgment against the buyer on Jan. 16, 2026, then filed writs and garnishee summonses. As of the court's Oct. 23, 2026 decision, CNRL had recovered nothing. (2026 ABKB 742, paras. 9, 76, 82–84.)

## What the court records say about the deal

- **The price was mostly assumed liabilities.** Justice Feasby: "much of the deal consideration is comprised of assumed liabilities that would otherwise be transferred to the Orphan Well Association" (2025 ABKB 621, para. 24). The receiver said this was the only bid in the remarketing process that would not send properties to the OWA (para. 26).
- **Eleven months later, the AER put all of Sumerian's sites in the OWA's care anyway** (AER order, Oct. 5, 2026).
- **Crown arrears.** Cleo owed Alberta $240,000 ($170,000 royalties, $70,000 rent). The court refused to let $150,000 of pre-filing arrears be left in ResidualCo while Cleo kept the leases (2025 ABKB 621, paras. 29–38).
- **Former employees.** After the RVO, the federal minister ruled Cleo's former employees ineligible for Wage Earner Protection Program payments and moved to recover them. The court declined to intervene (2026 ABKB 742, paras. 31, 73).
- **Municipalities.** The MD of Wainwright No. 61, the MD of Provost No. 52, Flagstaff County, the County of Paintearth No. 18 and Vulcan County were creditors of the estate and shared the remaining surplus (about $164,000) with the AER under a priority settlement (2026 ABKB 742, paras. 91, 125, appearances).
- **Buyer's representations in the share purchase agreement (s. 6.2):** among others, that it is not insolvent (g); that it is dealing at arm's length with the receiver and the company (i); that it and its affiliates have under $100 million in Canadian assets and revenue (m); and that it "is a WTO investor within the meaning of the Investment Canada Act and is not a state-owned enterprise" (n).
- **Sealed appendix.** The unredacted agreement (price, deposit) was filed as Confidential Appendix C, sealed until the receiver certified closing (Order, Oct. 17, 2025, para. 5). The deal closed Nov. 19, 2025.

## What we have not established

- Who owns or controls 2698902 Alberta Corp., or who its directors are.
- Whether the "WTO investor" representation is accurate, and whether an Investment Canada Act notification was filed.
- Whether anyone from Cleo's former ownership is involved with the buyer. Nothing in the record we have says so.
- Whether Sumerian itself is in any insolvency proceeding. We found none. The company in bankruptcy is ResidualCo.

## Questions put or to be put

1. **To Sumerian / 2698902 Alberta Corp.** Who are the shareholders and directors of 2698902 Alberta Corp.? Is any controlling shareholder non-Canadian? Has the CNRL judgment been paid?
2. **To ISED.** Was an Investment Canada Act notification filed for the acquisition of Cleo Energy Corp. (Nov. 2025)? (Access request.)
3. **To A&M.** Is Confidential Appendix C now unsealed? What due diligence did the receiver do on the buyer's ownership and financial capacity?
4. **To the AER.** Did it assess the buyer's capability before telling the receiver in Sept. 2025 that it did not object?
5. **To the MD of Wainwright.** What did Cleo owe, and does Sumerian owe property taxes now?
