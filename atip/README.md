# Access-to-information releases

Records released to Prime Rogue Inc. under the federal *Access to Information Act* and Alberta's *Access to Information Act* concerning the October 17, 2026 CFB Wainwright fire.

**Licence notice.** These are government records. Prime Rogue grants no licence over them. They are reproduced for news reporting and public-interest purposes; Crown copyright may apply.

**Policy.**

- Releases are published in full within 72 hours of receipt, with the requester's personal identifiers removed.
- The only other redaction: information released by accident that graphically harms an individual's privacy (for example, an identifiable person's injuries). Any such redaction is marked on the page and noted here.
- Each release is hashed and timestamped (OpenTimestamps) on publication.

**Layout.** One folder per request: `atip/<institution>/<request-number>/` containing the release package, the cover letter, and a `NOTES.md` with dates, exemptions claimed and any complaint filed.
