Background

On July 28, 2026 (the “Bankruptcy Date”), 2755857 Alberta Ltd. (“ResidualCo”) was assigned
into bankruptcy by the Receiver (defined below) as authorized pursuant to the Claims Procedure
Order granted by the Court of King’s Bench of Alberta (the “Court”) on February 10, 2026 (the
“Claims Procedure Order”). Alvarez & Marsal Canada Inc. (“A&M”) was appointed as the
Licensed Insolvency Trustee (the “Trustee”) of ResidualCo by the Official Receiver, subject to
affirmation by the creditors of the Trustee’s appointment or substitution of another trustee by the
creditors of ResidualCo. The proceedings commenced upon ResidualCo's assignment into
bankruptcy are referred to herein as the “Bankruptcy Proceedings”. ResidualCo is now
considered a bankrupt entity.

The Notice of Intention to File a Proposal and Receivership Proceedings

In late 2024, Cleo Energy Corp. (“Cleo”) began experiencing financial difficulties due to several
challenges, including declining production, insufficient capital to undertake necessary repairs, and
thefts of critical infrastructure. These circumstances resulted in Cleo being unable to meet its
obligations as they became due. Consequently, on December 8, 2024, Cleo filed a Notice of
Intention to File a Proposal (“NOI”) pursuant to section 50.4(1) of the Bankruptcy and Insolvency
Act, RSC 1985, c B-3 (the “BIA”), and A&M was appointed as proposal trustee (the “Proposal
Trustee”) in the proceedings commenced under the NOI (the “NOI Proceedings”).

During the NOI Proceedings, Cleo continued to operate its business while conducting a Court-
approved sale and solicitation process (“SISP”) for its oil and gas properties. Several transactions
involving certain of Cleo’s properties were approved and closed; however, Cleo retained a number
of unsold properties (the “Remaining Assets”) with associated liabilities and ongoing operating
costs at the conclusion of the SISP. As a result, on June 2, 2025, the Court granted a Receivership
Order (the “Receivership Order”) pursuant to section 243(1) of the BIA and section 13(2) of the
Judicature Act, RSA 2000, c J-2whereby effective June 3, 2025, A&M was appointed receiver and
manager of Cleo (the “Receiver”) in the proceedings commenced under the Receivership Order
(the “Receivership Proceedings”).

During the Receivership Proceedings, the Receiver continued to realize on the Remaining Assets
and ultimately entered into a share purchase agreement with 2698902 Alberta Corporation for the
sale of the shares of Cleo, including its Remaining Assets. On November 14, 2025, the Court
granted a Reverse Vesting Order (the “RVO”) approving the transaction. The transaction
subsequently closed on November 19, 2025. Pursuant to the RVO, certain excluded assets and
liabilities were transferred to ResidualCo, Cleo was discharged from the Receivership
Proceedings, and ResidualCo was substituted in its place.

All publicly available information with respect to the NOI Proceedings and the Receivership
Proceedings, including the Proposal Trustee’s and Receiver’s reports, Court orders and application
materials, are available on A&M’s website at www.alvarezandmarsal.com/cleo (the “Website”).
Materials relating to the Bankruptcy Proceedings can also be found on the Website.

Potential Conflict of Interest

A&M is not aware of any potential conflicts of interest with respect to it acting as Trustee in the
bankruptcy of ResidualCo. As discussed, A&M currently acts as the Receiver of ResidualCo.

Conservatory & Protective Measures and Preliminary Evaluation of Assets

As at the Bankruptcy Date, the Trustee confirmed that there are no realizable assets within
ResidualCo and that the ResidualCo bank account balance is $0.

The Trustee maintains a trust account with the Royal Bank of Canada, and the Receiver has
guaranteed the costs associated with administering the bankruptcy of ResidualCo.

Books and Records

The Receiver currently has access to the books and records of ResidualCo. As previously discussed,
the Receiver and ResidualCo will make available all books and records, as required, to the Trustee
and will deliver all books and records to the Trustee once the Receiver has been discharged by the
Court of its duties pursuant to the Claims Procedure Order.
