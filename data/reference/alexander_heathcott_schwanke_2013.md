Used in working model v4 for:
- Head fire rate of spread suppression classes for grass fires: < 1.0 km/h easy; 1-4.9 km/h challenging; >= 5.0 km/h beyond control (section 4).
- Benchmark: Lethbridge fire, average head spread 138 m/min (8.3 km/h) observed vs 145 m/min (8.7 km/h) predicted by FBP O-1b at 100% curing, ISI 42, 10 m winds averaging 69 km/h (section 4.1).
- Grass-fire length-to-breadth ratio L:B = 1.1 x WS^0.464 (Table A1.1, adapted from McArthur 1966 and Cheney 1981), the relation used in our spread model since part 1.
- Context: "Fires in cured grass are especially responsive to sudden changes in the strength and direction of the wind" (front matter).
