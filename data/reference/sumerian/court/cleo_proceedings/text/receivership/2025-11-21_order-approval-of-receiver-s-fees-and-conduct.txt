           approving the fees and disbursements of the Receiver and its legal counsel;

                    AND UPON HAVING READ the Application and the Second Report of the Receiver dated
           November 5, 2025 (the “Second Report”);

                    AND UPON HAVING READ the Affidavit of Service of Marica Ceko sworn November 12,

       IT IS HEREBY ORDERED AND DECLARED THAT:

SERVICE


1.     Service of notice of this application and supporting materials is hereby declared to be good
       and sufficient, no other person is required to have been served with notice of this
       application and time for service of this application is abridged to that actually given.


RECEIVER’S ACTIVITIES AND PROFESSIONAL FEES


2.     The Receiver’s actions, activities, and conduct as disclosed and reported in the Second
       Report are hereby ratified and approved.


3.     The Receiver’s accounts for fees and disbursements, as set out in the Second Report, are
       hereby approved without the necessity of a formal passing or assessment of its accounts.

4.     The accounts of the Receiver’s legal counsel, Miller Thomson LLP, for its fees and
       disbursements, as set out in the Second Report, are hereby approved without the
       necessity of a formal passing or assessment of its accounts.
