# Manual NASA FIRMS downloads, Oct. 17–18, 2026 (package v2)

Prime Rogue's first evidence package: KMZ/KML files downloaded by hand from NASA FIRMS between 11:50 p.m. MDT Oct. 17 and 10:15 p.m. MDT Oct. 18, 2026, before the automated archive (`data/firms/raw/`) existed. Covers Canada and Alberta, all sensors, with 24 h to 7-day windows (Oct. 12–18).

**Preserved as published.** `README.txt` and every data file are byte-for-byte the v2 package prepared Oct. 19, 2026; `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt` verifies them. Nothing here has been edited.

**Read with later findings in mind:**
- `README.txt` refers to "the explosion," reflecting early reports on Oct. 17–18. Those reports are unconfirmed; the Canadian Armed Forces have since said there is no evidence of an explosion or gas-infrastructure involvement.
- The detections in this package match the automated archive (`data/firms/detections.csv`), which also has later and fuller data.
