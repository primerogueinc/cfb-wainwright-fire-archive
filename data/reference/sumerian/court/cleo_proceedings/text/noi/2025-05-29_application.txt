Remedy claimed or sought:

1.     The Applicant, Alvarez & Marsal Canada Inc., in its capacity as Proposal Trustee (in
       such capacity, the “Proposal Trustee”), of CLEO Energy Corp. (“CLEO”) seeks an
       Order substantially in the form attached as Schedule “A”, among other things:

       (a)    abridging the time for service of this application and deeming service good and
              sufficient, if necessary;

       (b)    approving the actions, activities and conduct of the Proposal Trustee, as set out
              in the Fifth Report of the Proposal Trustee, dated April 24, 2025 (the “Fifth
              Report”) and the Sixth Report of the Proposal Trustee, dated May 26, 2025
              (the “Sixth Report”, and together with the Fifth Report, the “Reports”);

       (c)    approving the fees and disbursements of the Proposal Trustee and its legal
              counsel, Miller Thomson LLP (“Miller Thomson”), as set out in the Reports; and

       (d)    granting such further and other relief as counsel may request and this
              Honourable Court may deem appropriate.

Grounds for making this Application:

Background

2.     On December 8, 2025, CLEO filed a notice of intention to make a proposal pursuant to
       Division I of Part III of the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act, RSC 1985, c B-, commencing
       proposal proceedings (the “Proposal Proceedings”).

3.     The Proposal Trustee consented to act as proposal trustee in the Proposal Proceedings.

Actions, Activities and Conduct

4.     The Proposal Trustee seeks approval of its actions, activities and conduct.

5.     The Proposal Trustee’s actions, activities and conduct as detailed in the Reports, is
       appropriate and reasonable and should be approved by this Honourable Court.

Fees and Disbursements

6.     The Proposal Trustee seeks approval of its professional fees and disbursements as well
       as those of its legal counsel, Miller Thomson.
